Home cooking, without the wait

Casual Kitchen Express cooks home-style Nigerian meals in Ugbowo, Benin City, and gets them to you fast.

Why we started

Near the University of Benin, good food usually means a long wait, and quick food rarely tastes like home. We wanted both: jollof that tastes like a family party, soup cooked the way someone’s mother would, and a kitchen organised enough to get it to you while it’s still hot.

Questions

Straight answers about ordering, delivery, and the app.

What is Casual Kitchen Express?

A kitchen in Ugbowo, Benin City, cooking home-style Nigerian meals and delivering them fast. You order in our Android app or walk in at the counter.

Where do you deliver, and where do you stop?

From Uselu to Oluku Market, including Ugbowo, Ekosodin, BDPA estate, Osasogie, and Isihor. We don’t deliver beyond Oluku Market, but you can order for pickup from anywhere.

How much does delivery cost?

A flat ₦1,500 per order, whatever you order. Pickup is free.

What are your opening hours?

Online orders and delivery run from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm. Walk-in service runs until 8:30 pm.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, at the counter until 8:30 pm. You can pay with cash or online.

How do I install the app?

Download the installer from the App page, open it, and allow your browser to install apps if your phone asks. The App page shows each step.

Which Android versions work?

Android 10 or newer.

Why is the app not on the Play Store, and is it safe?

For now we share it directly. Your phone may warn you that it comes from an unknown source, which is normal for apps installed this way. Only download it from this website or our GitHub release page.

How do I know the file is genuine?

Each release lists a checksum on the release page. If you know how, compare it with the file you downloaded.

How do I track my order and use the delivery code?

Open Orders in the app to see the timeline. Your 4-digit code is shown there. Give it to your rider at the door to confirm delivery.

Do I pay in the app?

Yes, by card or bank transfer when you order. The app currently uses demo payments, and no money is taken.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile in the app and tap Delete my account at the bottom.

How do I get updates?

The app tells you when a new version is ready and opens the release page, so you can install it over the old one.

Privacy

What we collect, why, and how to remove it. Last updated November 2026.

What we collect from customers

  • Your name and email, from your Google account when you sign in, so we know whose order it is.
  • Your delivery address, map pin, landmark, and phone number, so the rider can find you.
  • Your gender, only if you choose to give it.
  • A profile photo, only if you add one. It’s compressed on your phone before upload.
  • Your orders, so you can track and reorder them.

Payments

The app currently uses demo payments. No real card or bank details are collected or stored.

Location

We don’t track customers. Riders share their location only while they’re carrying an order, so you can see your food on the way.

Staff

For our riders and kitchen staff we keep a name, phone number, staff ID, and optionally gender, home address, and a photo. Only managers and the person themself can see these details.

Deleting your data

Delete your account from Profile in the app. We remove your name, email, addresses, and photo. Past orders stay in our records without your details.
