Questions

Straight answers about ordering, delivery, and the app.

What is Casual Kitchen Express?

A kitchen in Ugbowo, Benin City, cooking home-style Nigerian meals and delivering them fast. You order in our Android app or walk in at the counter.

Where do you deliver, and where do you stop?

From Uselu to Oluku Market, including Ugbowo, Ekosodin, Uwelu, BDPA estate, Osasogie, and Isihor. We don’t deliver beyond Oluku Market, but you can order for pickup from anywhere.

How much does delivery cost?

A flat ₦1,500 per order, whatever you order. Pickup is free.

What are your opening hours?

We’re open every day. Online orders and delivery run from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm. Walk-in service runs until 8:30 pm.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, at the counter until 8:30 pm. You can pay with cash or online.

How do I install the app?

Download the installer from the App page, open it, and allow your browser to install apps if your phone asks. The App page shows each step.

Which Android versions work?

Android 10 or newer.

Why is the app not on the Play Store, and is it safe?

For now we share it directly. Your phone may warn you that it comes from an unknown source, which is normal for apps installed this way. Only download it from this website or our GitHub release page.

How do I know the file is genuine?

Each release lists a SHA-256 checksum on the release page and on our App page. If you know how, compare it with the file you downloaded.

How do I track my order and use the delivery code?

Open Orders in the app to see the timeline. Your 4-digit code is shown there. Give it to your rider at the door to confirm delivery.

Do I pay in the app?

Yes, by card or bank transfer when you order. The app currently uses demo payments, and no money is taken.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile in the app and tap Delete my account at the bottom. Our privacy page says what is removed.

How do I get updates?

The app tells you when a new version is ready and opens the release page, so you can install it over the old one.
