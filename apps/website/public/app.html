Everything it does

Ordering

  • Browse the full menu without an account.
  • Dishes come with their usual options, so one tap adds them.
  • Delivery for a flat ₦1,500, or free pickup.
  • Sign in with Google at checkout, with no password to remember.
  • Pay by card or bank transfer (demo payments for now).

Tracking

  • A timeline from the moment the kitchen has your order.
  • A notification at each step, even with the app closed.
  • Your rider’s name and position on the map once the food is on its way.
  • A 4-digit code that confirms delivery.

Your account

  • Saved addresses with a map pin and a landmark, or one tap to use where you are.
  • Order history and reorder in two taps.
  • Light or dark theme.
  • Delete your account from inside the app.

Download for Android

The download starts straight away. Release notes and the checksum are on our release page on GitHub.

  1. Download the installer file. Tap the button above and download the latest release.
  2. Open the file. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  3. Continue if you see a warning. Your phone may say the app comes from an unknown source. That’s because it’s installed directly rather than through the Play Store.
  4. Open the app and browse the menu. You only sign in when you check out.
