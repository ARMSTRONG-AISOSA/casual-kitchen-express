Privacy

What we collect, why, and how to remove it. Last updated 16 November 2026.

This covers the Casual Kitchen Express customer app, rider app, and business app, and this website. We collect only what we need to cook and deliver your food. We don’t sell your details, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

What we collect from customers

  • Your name, email address, and Google profile picture, from your Google account when you sign in, so we know whose order it is.
  • Your phone number and delivery addresses (area, map pin, and landmark), so the rider can find you and call if needed.
  • Your gender, only if you choose to give it.
  • A profile photo, only if you add one. It’s made smaller on your phone before upload.
  • Your orders and ratings, so you can track and reorder, and so the kitchen can improve.
  • A notification token for your phone, so we can tell you when your order moves on.

Payments

The app currently uses demo payments. No real card or bank details are collected or stored, and no money is taken.

Location

Customers: the app reads your location only when you tap Use my current location, once, to place your map pin. We keep only the pin you save.

Riders: while carrying an order, the rider app shares the rider’s position about every 45 seconds, with a notification showing the whole time, so the customer can follow the food on the map. Only that customer sees it. Sharing stops, and the position is deleted, when the delivery ends or the rider goes offline.

Notifications

Notifications reach your phone through Expo’s push service and Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging. They carry order updates only. Your phone’s token is removed when you sign out.

Staff

For our riders and kitchen staff we keep a name, phone number, staff ID, and optionally gender, home address, and a photo. Only managers and the person themself can see these details.

Services we use

  • Google, for sign-in and for delivering notifications (Firebase).
  • Expo, for sending notifications to the apps.
  • Cloudflare, which runs our server and stores photos.
  • Neon, which hosts our database.
  • OpenFreeMap, for the map. Your phone loads map images from their servers, so they see your IP address, as any website does.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website and the app downloads.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while your account is open. If you deactivate your account, it’s hidden until you sign in and reactivate it.

Deleting your data

Delete your account from Profile in the app, with Delete my account. We remove your name, email, phone number, addresses, photo, and notification tokens. Past orders stay in our records without your details, for the kitchen’s accounts. You can also ask us to delete your data using the contacts below.

This website

The site sets no cookies. If you change the theme, your choice is kept in your own browser. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, and the App page asks GitHub for the latest release, so both see your IP address. GitHub, which hosts the site, may keep visit logs.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
