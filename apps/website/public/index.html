How ordering works

From your phone to your door in three steps

  1. Order in a few taps

    Browse the menu without signing up. The usual options are already chosen, so most dishes take one tap.

  2. Track it live

    Follow your order from the kitchen to your door, and see your rider on the map once the food is on its way.

  3. Give your rider the code

    Your 4-digit code confirms the food reached you, and nobody else.

The app

Built for busy days near campus

Students and young workers around the University of Benin told us the same thing: they want real food, quickly, without fuss. So the app does a few things very well.

All app features

  • Browse first, sign in later

    See every dish and price before you commit. Sign in with Google only when you check out.

  • Reorder in two taps

    Your last order waits at the top of the menu. Tap Reorder, then Pay.

  • Live tracking and a delivery code

    Know when the kitchen has your order, when it’s packed, and when your rider is close.

  • Card or bank transfer

    Pay in the app when you order. The app currently uses demo payments, and no money is taken.

Before you download

Read all the questions

Why isn’t the app on the Play Store?

For now we share it directly, from this website and from our release page. Your phone may warn you that it comes from an unknown source. The App page shows each step of the install.

Where do you deliver?

From Uselu to Oluku Market, including Ugbowo, Ekosodin, Uwelu, BDPA estate, Osasogie, and Isihor. We don’t deliver beyond Oluku Market, but you can always order for pickup.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones running Android 10 or newer.
