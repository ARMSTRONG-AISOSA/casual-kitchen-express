Home cooking, without the wait

Casual Kitchen Express cooks home-style Nigerian meals in Ugbowo, Benin City, and gets them to you fast.

Why we started

Near the University of Benin, good food usually means a long wait, and quick food rarely tastes like home. We wanted both: jollof that tastes like a family party, soup cooked the way someone’s mother would, and a kitchen organised enough to get it to you while it’s still hot.

What we serve

Rice meals: jollof, fried rice, or a combo of both from ₦2,000
Soup of the day with eba or pounded yam ₦3,000
Proteins and sides: chicken, beef, fish, plantain, moi moi from ₦600
Snacks: meat pie, spring rolls, doughnuts from ₦500
Combos with a drink from ₦4,100

Prices are examples for now and may change.

Where and when

Our kitchen is in Ugbowo, close to the University of Benin, and open every day. Online orders and delivery run from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm. Walk in until 8:30 pm and pay at the counter with cash or online.

Delivery area

We deliver from Uselu to Oluku Market for a flat ₦1,500, along the Benin–Lagos road and the streets either side: Uselu, Uwelu, Evbogida, Ugbowo, Ekosodin, BDPA estate, Osasogie, Isihor, Idunmwowina, Iguosa, and Oluku. Beyond Oluku Market, you can still order for pickup. The app checks your map pin when you add an address.

What we care about

  • Real taste. Recipes that taste like home, not like a shortcut.
  • Speed. A short menu and a tidy kitchen, so food leaves fast.
  • Consistency. The same portion and the same flavour every time.
  • Fair prices. Affordable for students and young workers.
